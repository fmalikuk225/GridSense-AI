# Energy-sector business case

## Intended audience
Energy analytics, demand planning, commercial forecasting and digital transformation teams, including the types of teams found in electricity networks and energy generation companies.

## Value hypothesis
An accessible forecasting dashboard could help analysts identify demand peaks and compare prediction approaches before considering investment in a more robust forecasting service.

## Evidence boundary
No real-world savings, emissions reductions, reliability improvements or commercial impact have been measured. These are hypotheses to validate with real datasets, stakeholder interviews and operational baselines.

## Production gap
A production system needs source validation, secure data governance, horizon-specific rolling backtests, uncertainty estimates, monitoring, audit logs, incident procedures, access controls and operational integration.
